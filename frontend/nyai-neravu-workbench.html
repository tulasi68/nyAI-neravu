Proposed platform · end-to-end concept

From legal question to a reviewable, source-linked decision package.

Organise the record, connect legal sources, hear both sides online, run independent analysis lanes, prepare a draft, and leave the final decision to the authorised human authority.

What Nyai-Neravu is intended to become

Concept stage
Knowledge layer
Law, linked and versioned

Constitution, statutes, rules and judgments with source provenance.

Case layer
Every claim linked to a record

Documents, chronology, party positions, evidence and missing information.

Challenge layer
Independent scrutiny

Separate analytical tasks identify contrary arguments and unsupported claims.

Authority layer
Human decision-maker stays in control

AI prepares reviewable material. Only a legally authorised authority decides.
