What Nyai-Neravu is intended to become

A connected legal knowledge and case-preparation platform — not a chatbot dressed as a judge.
Concept stage
Knowledge layer
Law, linked and versioned

Constitution, statutes, rules and judgments with source provenance, amendments and court hierarchy.

Case layer
Every claim linked to a record

Documents, chronology, party positions, evidence references, disputed facts and missing information.

Challenge layer
Independent scrutiny, not majority vote

Separate analytical tasks identify contrary arguments, unsupported assertions and citation problems.

Authority layer
Human decision-maker stays in control

AI prepares reviewable material. Only a legally authorised authority can make a binding determination.

Stage 04

Online party hearing (mock)

Parties submit arguments and evidence online. The system records questions and responses. No real hearing is conducted.
Simulated exchange
Party A (Claimant) — written submission
The respondent accepted our advance of ₹4,50,000 under the agreement dated 10 Jan 2025 (Ex.P1, Ex.P2). Despite repeated demands (Ex.P3), no goods were delivered. We seek refund with interest. We dispute the authenticity of the unsigned delivery challan (Ex.D2).
Party B (Respondent) — written response
Goods were delivered on or about 28 Feb 2025. The delivery challan (Ex.D2) and our reply letter (Ex.D1) record this. The claim for refund is an afterthought. We deny liability and request dismissal.
Nyai-Neravu (neutral questions — mock)
1. Does either party produce a signed acknowledgment of receipt of goods?
2. What is the contractual delivery timeline and place stated in Ex.P1?
3. Has each party had an opportunity to respond to the documents relied upon by the other?

Stage 07

Human legal / judicial review

The human reviewer may reject, return, revise or independently decide. The AI does not sign or issue a judgment.
No legal effect

Review checklist

Original record
Must be checked against official materials.
Both parties’ arguments
Check coverage and opportunity to respond.
Legal authorities
Verify existence, current status and proposition support.
Unresolved issues
Resolve or explicitly record why they remain open.

Reviewer decision (demo only)

Selecting a review action only records a local demonstration event. It does not constitute approval, judicial determination or a valid court order.

What is still missing
Everything that makes it real

Verified legal corpus, document authentication, durable storage, identity/RBAC, real multi-model analysis, court integration, and a lawful adjudicatory framework.

Higher-court or appellate review is intentionally left outside this mock. Any future “Nyai-Neravu higher court” would face the same requirements of law, institution and human authority.
This prototype does not call AI models, search legal databases, persist case records to a backend, notify parties or connect to a court. All sample content is fictional. A working demonstration of screens is not operational readiness, institutional approval or legal authority.
