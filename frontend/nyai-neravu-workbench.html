What Nyai-Neravu is intended to become

A connected legal knowledge and case-preparation platform—not a chatbot dressed as a judge.
Concept stage
Knowledge layer
Law, linked and versioned

Constitutional provisions, legislation, rules and judgments with source provenance, amendments, court hierarchy and later treatment.

Case layer
Every claim linked to a record

Documents, chronology, party positions, evidence references, disputed facts, missing information and source coverage.

Challenge layer
Independent scrutiny, not majority vote

Separate analytical tasks identify contrary arguments, unsupported assertions, citation problems and uncertainty.

Authority layer
Human decision-maker stays in control

AI prepares reviewable material. Only a legally authorised authority can make a binding determination under applicable procedure.

The current repository does not yet contain a comprehensive Indian legal corpus, live authoritative retrieval, independent multi-vendor analysis, authenticated evidence handling or court integration.
